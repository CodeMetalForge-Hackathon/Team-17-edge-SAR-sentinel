Corner order: BL=(0,0) green, BR=(1,0) blue, TR=(1,1) orange, TL=(0,1) magenta (as seen in the image). Cells: letter = column left→right, number = row bottom→top (A1 is bottom-left). All coordinates, footprint sizes and margins are dimensionless fractions of the boundary, NOT metres. Lens distortion is NOT corrected, so the mapping is only approximately straight-line accurate, worse toward image edges. The sandbox's real aspect ratio is unknown, so a "radius" in normalized units is only an approximation of a circle. Planning inflates every obstacle by the rover footprint's enclosing-circle radius plus the extra margin and keeps the rover centre that far from the sandbox edge (whole-cell test: conservative, finer grids allow narrower gaps). Obstacle footprints come from Detect rubble (current photo vs the saved BEFORE photo; detected ones are drawn dashed) and can be corrected by hand. This is a static route preview: not a guarantee of physical clearance, not a motor command. Rover forward direction and motion conventions are UNVERIFIED until tested on hardware. Each move needs a fresh photo and re-set pose.
