## Scope
1. `pi/damage_context.py`: `load_context(ctx_path, assoc_path)` validates against the schema in the feasibility plan and raises `ValueError` on: wrong `status` (must be `CACHED`), unknown `kind`, missing provenance, `association` not `STAGED_MANUAL`, or a zone naming an unknown sample. `context_message(ctx, assoc)` flattens to `{zones: [...]}`, where each zone has `zone`, `name`, `polygon`, `sample_id`, `summary_class`, `kind`, `status`, `association`, plus top-level provenance.
2. `bus.py`: `publish(topic, payload, retain=False)`. `forge/context/damage` is retained.
3. `dashboard.py`: `self.context`, a panel with the labels "CACHED", the kind, and "STAGED BY OPERATOR", zones drawn distinct from hazard circles. Reset keeps it.
4. Real files in `demo_assets/` from ML-01, reviewed by a human.
